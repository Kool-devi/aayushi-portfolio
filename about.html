About Aayushi

I turn complex product problems into clear, useful experiences.

I’m a product designer who works from the system outward—understanding how people think, what the product needs to do, and where better structure can make both feel simpler.

My work moves between research, product strategy, interaction design, prototyping, and development-ready systems.

How I work

Clarity first. Craft with a reason.

I’ve led product design at early-stage startups and worked on digital products for global clients. I’m most useful when a product has grown complicated, the path forward is unclear, or a team needs to turn scattered insight into a coherent experience.

I treat each brief as a systems problem. That means listening closely, making the invisible structure visible, testing ideas in context, and carrying decisions through to the details that ship.

A few constants

What I bring to the work

  1. 01

    Research that changes the work

    Evidence becomes useful when it sharpens priorities and gives the team something concrete to act on.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Prototypes and clear design rationale help teams discuss the real product, not an abstract idea of it.
